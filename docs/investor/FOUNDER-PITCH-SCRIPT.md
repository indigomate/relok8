# Relok8 pre-seed pitch script

Use this as a 7–9 minute talk track. It follows the editable deck slide by slide. Do not present roadmap features as live, and replace any editing note only with verified metrics.

## 1. Cover — 20 seconds

Relok8 is building the financial operating layer for moving home. We begin with lease takeovers, where urgency, trust and coordination problems are already acute. The live product handles discovery and the handover workflow today. We then add regulated financial rails through licensed partners.

International students are the focused entry market, not the limit of the platform. Relok8 is designed for foreign nationals, expats, workers, families and people moving locally as well.

## 2. Problem — 35 seconds

Moving rental homes is already a financial transaction, but the workflow is fragmented. People discover a listing in one place, message in another, validate identity informally, seek landlord approval manually and move deposits or rent without shared status. International renters experience the same problem with an additional language and local-trust gap.

## 3. Beachhead — 30 seconds

Lease takeovers give each participant a reason to complete the transaction. The outgoing tenant wants to avoid double rent. The incoming tenant wants a trustworthy home. The landlord wants to approve the right person without extra administration. Relok8 coordinates all three and creates the record that future financial products can attach to.

## 4. Product — 40 seconds

This is live at relok8.online. Users can discover and publish listings, save favourites, make enquiries, message, invite a landlord and coordinate a handover. The platform also has multilingual architecture and lifecycle emails. We are deliberately not claiming live payments or escrow today.

## 5. Workflow — 35 seconds

Relok8 turns the move into a staged transaction: find, verify, coordinate, approve, settle and hand over. Discovery, coordination and landlord approval are the live foundation. Verification, documented completion and partner-led settlement are the next layers.

## 6. FinPropTech roadmap — 45 seconds

Phase one is the marketplace and coordination layer. Phase two adds identity handoff, deposit or rent initiation and partner services through licensed providers. Phase three becomes the B2B operating layer for landlords, residences and other housing operators. Licensed partners hold and safeguard funds; Relok8 owns the consented workflow and transaction status.

## 7. Business model — 35 seconds

The model is deliberately simple: 49 once and 49 monthly. An incoming tenant pays 49 zloty only after the landlord accepts the reservation. The landlord then pays 49 zloty for each month that tenancy remains active. Search, listing, messaging, live translation and the handover workspace are included. Optional upside can later come from insurance, utilities, connectivity, moving and FX partners; institutional plans; partner-led payment services; and API usage. None of those is required to access the core platform.

## 8. Revenue potential — 45 seconds

At an illustrative ten paid landlord months, each tenancy bills 539 zloty gross: 49 once plus ten times 49. After an illustrative 23% VAT and a model exchange rate of 4.30 zloty per euro, that is about €102 net revenue per tenancy. Twenty thousand annual tenancies produce roughly €2.0 million; 250,000 produce €25.5 million; and 1.5 million produce approximately €153 million. These base scenarios deliberately exclude optional partner, institutional, payments and API revenue.

## 9. Market and expansion — 40 seconds

We use international students as a measurable entry market, not as the boundary of Relok8. Poland had 108,609 international students in 2024/25. The EU had 1.65 million students from abroad in 2024, up 4% year over year, and UNESCO reports nearly 7.3 million students studying abroad globally in 2023. That cohort helps us prove density and multilingual trust. The same workflow then expands to other foreign nationals, workers, expats, families and local renters.

## 10. Go-to-market — 40 seconds

The strategy is city density, not a broad national launch. We seed supply through outgoing tenants, residences, housing groups and landlord portfolios. We capture high-intent demand around lease exits and city searches. We concierge the first completed handovers, then convert repeated workflows into paid institutional pilots. The north-star metric is landlord-approved handovers per active listing.

## 11. Competitive position — 30 seconds

Portals own discovery, property software owns landlord operations, payment providers own financial rails and messaging tools own conversation. Relok8’s position is the multi-party move workflow connecting those fragments. We do not need to replace every specialist; we need to become the orchestration layer users and partners rely on.

## 12. Defensibility — 30 seconds

AI translation by itself is not the moat. The defensibility compounds through completed moves: a consented workflow graph, local marketplace density, trust signals, partner integrations and consistent multilingual communication.

## 13. What is de-risked — 40 seconds

The production app, authenticated workflows, messaging, email delivery, listing controls and translation architecture reduce technical risk. The next ninety days must prove marketplace repeatability: one dense cohort, 25 completed handovers, three institutional design partners and willingness to pay. Insert current traction only when the figures are verified.

## 14. Raise — 35 seconds

We are raising €250,000 for roughly eighteen months. The capital funds product and compliance, one or two focused city launches, trust operations and founder runway against measurable targets. The structure can combine a grant with angels or a specialist pre-seed investor.

## 15. Close — 20 seconds

Relok8 already has the product wedge. This round builds the evidence, distribution and regulated partnerships to turn it into FinPropTech infrastructure. We are looking for investors and partners with marketplace, property, payments or rental-mobility experience.

## Questions to prepare for

- Why will users switch from Facebook groups or listing portals?
- How will you acquire the first 100 high-quality listings in one city?
- What does a completed handover mean, and how will it be verified?
- Which organisation will pay first, how much and from which budget?
- Which licensed payment, KYC or insurance partners are realistic?
- How will landlord approval and tenancy-law differences be handled?
- What user data is necessary, what is consented and what is deleted?
- What are the verified current figures for users, active listings, enquiries and completed handovers?
- Why is this founder uniquely positioned to build Relok8?
