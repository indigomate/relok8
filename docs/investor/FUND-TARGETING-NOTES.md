# How to tailor the Relok8 master deck

Use the PowerPoint as the master file and duplicate it for each application. Keep the core facts identical; change the emphasis, examples and raise slide to match the reviewer.

## Pi Labs or another PropTech specialist

- Keep the “financial operating layer for moving home” category language.
- Spend more time on slides 5, 6 and 10: the workflow, partner-led financial rails and category position.
- Add any conversations with landlords, residences, property managers, payment providers or insurers.
- Make clear that licensed partners hold funds and handle regulated obligations.
- Best evidence: completed handovers, design-partner letters and a specific partner integration plan.

## Market One Capital or another marketplace investor

- Lead with the lease-takeover wedge and three-sided incentives.
- Add verified supply, demand, response-time and completion metrics to slide 12.
- Be ready to explain liquidity by city, repeat usage, referral behaviour and the path from transactions to revenue.
- Best evidence: active listings, qualified enquiries per listing, time to match, landlord approval rate and completed handovers.

## SMOK Ventures or another CEE pre-seed investor

- Emphasise Poland as a capital-efficient launch market and the repeatable city playbook.
- Show founder velocity: what was built, deployed and tested with limited capital.
- Explain why the product can expand beyond Poland without becoming a service-heavy relocation agency.
- Best evidence: fast iteration, low acquisition cost, organic channels and a short path to paid pilots.

## Seedcamp or another pan-European fund

- Emphasise cross-border mobility, multilingual workflow and the EU expansion logic.
- Add a specific second-country hypothesis after the Polish city playbook is proven.
- Show how partner integrations and workflow data become reusable infrastructure across markets.
- Best evidence: international user behaviour, partner demand and a credible regulatory/compliance sequencing plan.

## PARP or another non-dilutive programme

- Replace investor language with the programme’s own innovation, implementation and eligible-cost terminology.
- Resize the ask and allocation to the programme rules; do not assume all founder runway, marketing or partner costs are eligible.
- Turn slide 13 into work packages, outputs, milestones and measurable indicators.
- Keep the distinction between live product, research/development and commercial implementation.
- Confirm eligibility before applying, especially if the programme requires a specific incubation route or restricts ideas already operated commercially.

## Metrics to collect immediately

These numbers will improve almost every application:

1. Verified registered users.
2. Active, approved listings by city.
3. Qualified enquiries per active listing.
4. Median first-response time.
5. Matches that reached landlord invitation.
6. Landlord approvals.
7. Completed handovers.
8. Days from listing to completed handover.
9. Organic/referral share of acquisition.
10. Institutional discovery calls, design partners, pilots and letters of intent.

Never estimate these as current traction. Report verified counts, the measurement period and the data source.
