# Relok8 capital pipeline

**Prepared:** 23 November 2026  
**Target round:** €250,000 pre-seed  
**Current stage:** production product, pre-validation / pre-repeatability  
**Status:** research only; no applications or outreach have been submitted.

## Recommended order

### Tier 1 — act now

#### 1. PARP Start-ups Are Us — Smart City

- **Type:** non-dilutive training and foreign-market missions; not unrestricted cash.
- **Application window:** 24 November–8 December 2026.
- **Fit:** strong if Relok8 can credibly frame rental mobility, multilingual access and housing coordination as urban quality-of-life infrastructure.
- **Key eligibility:** Polish business operating for at least one year and no more than five years; MVP or final product; international expansion model; SME status.
- **Support:** PARP covers up to 100% of selected participation costs for Polish training and up to three foreign missions.
- **Important:** this does not replace founder runway.
- **Action:** confirm incorporation date and legal eligibility immediately; prepare Polish and English six-minute `.pptx` decks.
- **Official page:** https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/grants/grants/start-ups-are-us---branza-smart-city

#### 2. Pi Labs 2027 Growth Programme

- **Type:** equity/SAFE investment plus eight-week built-world growth programme.
- **Ticket:** £350,000 SAFE.
- **Status:** open; public Pi Labs communications indicate applications close 29 January 2027—confirm in the live form.
- **Fit:** strongest specialist match because Relok8 sits at property, rental workflow and AI-enabled communication.
- **Risk:** Pi Labs now explicitly emphasises AI/robotics and impressive metrics. Relok8 needs to make the AI/translation architecture and customer evidence concrete without overstating them.
- **Action:** clean the deck, add founder-market fit and submit once the first traction block is verified.
- **Official page:** https://sphere-hypersphere-pcac.squarespace.com/growth-programme

#### 3. Market One Capital

- **Type:** European pre-seed/seed VC; prefers to lead or co-lead.
- **Fit:** excellent thesis match for a marketplace or platform with network effects.
- **Access:** direct deck submissions are accepted; warm introductions are preferred but not required.
- **What they signal they want:** factual market insight, usage growth, engagement and network effects.
- **Action:** target after the editing notes are removed and one-city liquidity metrics are available.
- **Official FAQ and pitch route:** https://www.moc.vc/faq

#### 4. COBIN Angels

- **Type:** Polish business-angel network.
- **Published capital range:** PLN 100,000–2,000,000 per startup.
- **Fit:** the requested €250k round is within range, and angels may tolerate an earlier evidence stage than institutional VC.
- **Caveat:** COBIN publishes a 6% success fee on capital raised from investors it introduces. Review the agreement and economics with counsel before signing.
- **Action:** use only the clean send deck; request property, marketplace, fintech and payments angels.
- **Official startup page:** https://www.cobinangels.com/for-startups

### Tier 2 — apply after the first commercial proof

#### 5. SMOK Ventures

- **Type:** CEE pre-seed VC; often leads.
- **Published ticket:** US$100,000–1,000,000; historical average about US$300,000.
- **Fit:** Poland/CEE, proptech and marketplace are within scope; the €250k target is natural.
- **Current gap:** SMOK says first-time founders usually need customer validation or traction and that founders not fully focused on the company are a common reason for rejection.
- **Action:** approach after customer interviews, early approvals/handovers and a credible full-time transition plan.
- **Contact:** smok@smok.vc; short cold email plus PDF deck is acceptable.
- **Official FAQ:** https://www.smok.vc/faq/

#### 6. Seedcamp

- **Type:** European first-cheque pre-seed fund; rolling submissions.
- **Published first cheque:** US$350,000–1,250,000.
- **Fit:** cross-border European marketplace and financial-workflow ambition fit the fund’s category-building profile.
- **Current gap:** founder-market fit and global-scale evidence must be much stronger; the requested round is below their published normal first-cheque range.
- **Action:** pitch after the founder narrative and one-city validation evidence are strong; consider increasing the round only if the operating model justifies it.
- **Official FAQ:** https://seedcamp.com/faqs/
- **Pitch route:** https://seedcamp.com/out/f7-startup-details-core

#### 7. Digital Ocean Ventures

- **Type:** Poland/CEE pre-seed and seed VC focused on AI-powered digital transformation and fintech.
- **Published ticket:** PLN 1–5m, with up to PLN 3m initial.
- **Fit:** potentially strong when Relok8 has a credible B2B2C institutional product, AI translation evidence and partner-led financial workflow.
- **Current gap:** the present story is still primarily consumer marketplace; institutional pilots are needed.
- **Action:** approach after one or two residences, property operators or employers sign pilot/design-partner commitments.
- **Official pitch route:** https://dov.vc/pitch-your-startup

## First-close strategy

The fastest credible route is not one €250k cheque. Build a syndicate:

1. **Anchor:** €75k–€150k from a Polish/CEE angel or micro-VC.
2. **Specialist:** Pi Labs, Market One Capital or another property/marketplace investor.
3. **Follow-on angels:** €25k–€50k tickets from operators in property, marketplaces, payments and relocation.
4. **Non-dilutive support:** PARP for internationalisation expenses, not salary replacement.

Do not promise the founder will stop outside work before the round has closed and funds are in the company account. Investors will expect a clear full-time transition at or immediately after first close.
