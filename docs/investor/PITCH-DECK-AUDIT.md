# Relok8 pitch-deck audit

**Audit date:** 23 November 2026  
**Deck reviewed:** `Relok8-Pre-Seed-Pitch-Deck.pptx`  
**Purpose:** assess the quality of the fundraising narrative and evidence, not predict whether an investor will invest.

## Benchmark sources

The rubric is based on four established fundraising references:

1. [Sequoia Capital — Writing a Business Plan](https://sequoiacap.com/article/writing-a-business-plan): company purpose, problem, solution, why now, market, competition, business model, team, financials and vision.
2. [Y Combinator — A Guide to Seed Fundraising](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/4A-a-guide-to-seed-fundraising): product/demo, the right team, a very large outcome, traction, market size and minimum financials.
3. [Dropbox DocSend — Pitch deck design research](https://experience.dropbox.com/form/docsend/deck-design-report): investors may spend only about 2 minutes 42 seconds on a deck; problem, solution and why-now should flow together.
4. [OpenVC — How to Build a Startup Pitch Deck](https://www.openvc.app/blog/startup-pitch-deck): cover, problem, solution, product, traction, business model, market, go-to-market, team and ask.

### Previous version: approximately **71/100**

The earlier deck had a strong story and design, but lost points because it did not quantify the payer, pricing, revenue formula, revenue potential or post-Europe expansion. Correcting the founder name and adding transparent global scenarios improves the narrative by roughly 11 points.

## What the revenue scenarios mean

The deck now uses one explicit launch baseline:

`annual gross billed = approved reservations × 49 PLN + active landlord-tenancy months × 49 PLN`

| Scenario | Approved tenancies/year | Paid landlord months | Illustrative net annual revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Poland | 20,000 | 200,000 | €2.0m |
| Europe | 250,000 | 2,500,000 | €25.5m |
| Global | 1,500,000 | 15,000,000 | €152.9m |

These are **scenarios, not forecasts**. Each assumes one 49 PLN reservation confirmation, ten 49 PLN landlord payments, 23% illustrative VAT and a model conversion rate of 4.30 PLN/€. The pricing, average paid tenure, conversion, churn, refunds, VAT treatment and local-market adaptation must be tested.

The scenarios deliberately exclude optional future revenue from partner referrals, institutional plans, payment services and API usage. Student-mobility figures quantify the initial beachhead; they do not define the full addressable market.

## What prevents a 90+ score

### 1. Verified traction

Add a measured period and source for:

- registered and verified users;
- active listings by city;
- qualified enquiries per listing;
- landlord invitations and approvals;
- completed handovers;
- median time from listing to handover;
- repeat/referral acquisition;
- signed pilots, design partners or letters of intent.

Expected improvement: **+4 to +6 points**.

### 2. Indiana's founder-market fit

The deck needs two or three concrete sentences explaining:

- the experience or lived problem that produced the insight;
- why Indiana understands international renters, housing operations or financial workflows unusually well;
- what Indiana has built, sold, operated or learned that competitors cannot easily copy.

Expected improvement: **+3 to +5 points**.

### 3. Customer evidence

Include direct evidence from interviews or pilots: the number of interviews, a short anonymised insight, willingness-to-pay evidence and the next committed action. Do not use invented testimonials.

Expected improvement: **+2 to +4 points**.

### 4. Operating economics

Build a 24-month model covering headcount, city launch costs, acquisition, conversion, gross margin, partner attach, runway and downside cases. The slide deck should show only the key assumptions; the detailed model belongs in the data room.

Expected improvement: **+1 to +3 points**.

## Investor-readiness verdict

- **Visual and narrative quality:** ready for investor conversations.
- **Claims discipline:** strong; live and future capabilities are separated.
- **Institutional investability evidence:** incomplete.
- **Recommended use now:** warm introductions, accelerator applications and discovery meetings.
- **Recommended before a broad VC process:** verify traction, add founder-market fit, secure design partners and build the operating model.

No pitch deck can “guarantee” funding. The deck earns the next meeting; the company evidence, founder insight and execution determine the investment.
